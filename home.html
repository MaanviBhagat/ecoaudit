About us

EcoAudit is a web/mobile platform offering a guided, modular sustainability setup for individuals and households. While climate anxiety is rising among citizens, most individuals struggle with how to start making sustainable changes in their own homes and routines. Whether constructing a new home or trying to live more sustainably in an existing one, people lack access to customized, actionable guidance for eco-friendly upgrades or daily swaps.
